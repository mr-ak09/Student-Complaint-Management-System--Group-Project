## 📖 About The Project

Traditional grievance redressal within educational institutions relies heavily on manual, paper-based workflows that are slow, disorganized, and prone to record loss. 

The **Student Complaint Management System (SCMS)** provides a modern, transparent web application that bridges the communication gap between students and campus administrators. The platform enables students to lodge categorized complaints and monitor their progress in real-time, while empowering administrators with a centralized control center to review, delegate, and resolve issues efficiently.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 👨‍🎓 For Students
* **Easy Submission:** Responsive forms to register complaints across multiple categories (Academics, Infrastructure, Faculty/Staff, Financial Issues, etc.).
* **Real-Time Tracking:** Live status visibility (e.g., *Submitted*, *In Progress*, *Resolved*).
* **Feedback Mechanism:** Submit feedback and ratings once a complaint is marked resolved.

### 👨‍💼 For Administrators
* **Centralized Dashboard:** Single interface to view, filter, categorize, and prioritize grievances.
* **Delegation Tools:** Direct routing and task assignment to relevant departmental teams.
* **Resolution & Feedback Actions:** Approve, reject, or resolve complaints with attached administrative remarks.

### 🏛️ For Institutions
* **Operational Transparency:** End-to-end auditability and clear accountability across staff members.
* **Data Insights & Analytics:** Track trends and common bottlenecks to drive long-term policy improvements.

## 🔄 System Architecture & Workflow

1. **Student Registration/Login:** Students access the portal using their credentials.
2. **Lodge Complaint:** Fill in issue details, category, and relevant descriptions.
3. **Admin Queue & Delegation:** Admins review incoming tickets, categorize urgency, and assign them to departments.
4. **Resolution & Feedback:** Admins update the status; students receive confirmation and submit a resolution rating.
